Collections are a layer of organization that sits on top of your inventory. While your inventory is automatically structured by sport and category — populated directly from your Excel workbooks — Collections are curated by you, grouping cards however you see fit: by player, era, theme, set completion goal, or any other criteria that matters to your collection. A single card can belong to multiple collections at once, giving you total flexibility without duplicating data.

## Inventory vs. Collections

Your inventory and your collections serve different purposes and are managed in completely different ways.

| | Inventory | Collections |
|---|---|---|
| **How it's populated** | Automatically from Excel import | Manually by you |
| **Organization unit** | Sport/category (Basketball, Hockey, etc.) | Any grouping you define |
| **Card membership** | A card belongs to exactly one category | A card can belong to many collections |
| **Created by** | Import process | You, on the Manage Collections page |
| **Dirk Nowitzki PC** | Special category (card IDs 6000000–6999999) | Also appears as a hardcoded collection |

<Note>
The **Dirk Nowitzki Personal Collection** is a built-in, hardcoded collection based on card ID range 6000000–6999999. It appears alongside your user-created collections in the sidebar but cannot be renamed, deleted, or re-scoped. It always reflects every card in that ID range.
</Note>

## What a Collection Contains

Every collection you create has:

- **Name** — a short label that appears in the sidebar and on collection detail pages
- **Description** *(optional)* — free-text notes about the collection's purpose or scope
- **Cards** — any subset of your inventory, added one at a time or in bulk

Collections appear in the left sidebar under **Collections** as soon as you create them, giving you one-click access to any curated group from anywhere in the app.

## Collections and Goals

Collections are the backbone of the Goals system. When you link a Goal to a collection, CardVault automatically computes your progress — counting cards (Quantity goals) or summing their values (Value goals) — live from the database. You never have to update those numbers manually.
