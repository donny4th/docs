The Analytics Dashboard is your collection's command center. Every time you open it, CardVault computes live figures from your database and presents them in three stacked sections: high-level KPIs, composition tiles that break down what you own, and an interactive chart that plots how your collection has changed over time. Navigate there by clicking **Analytics → Dashboard** in the top navigation bar.

---

## Section 1 — KPI Tiles

Four tiles span the top of the page, each giving you a single, instantly readable number.

<CardGroup cols={4}>
  <Card title="Total Value" icon="dollar-sign">
    The sum of all card values across every category in your collection.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Total Cards" icon="layer-group">
    The raw count of every individual card record in the database.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Unique Card Types" icon="fingerprint">
    The number of distinct card definitions — multiple copies of the same card each add to Total Cards but count as one Unique Card Type.
  </Card>
  <Card title="CardVault 50 Index" icon="chart-line">
    The current index value, normalized to 100 at your first snapshot. Displays a **change vs. the prior snapshot** so you can see movement at a glance. Shows **—** until the first snapshot has been taken.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Section 2 — Composition Tiles

Three tiles sit below the KPIs and give you a structural picture of your collection.

### Graded Cards

This tile shows the total count of graded cards in your collection and your overall **gem rate** — the percentage of graded cards that meet the gem mint threshold:

| Grading Company | Gem Mint Boundary |
|---|---|
| PSA | Grade **10** or higher |
| SGC | Grade **10** or higher |
| CGC | Grade **10** or higher |
| TAG | Grade **10** or higher |
| AWG | Grade **10** or higher |
| BGS (Beckett) | Grade **9.5** or higher |

<Note>
  Beckett uses **9.5** as its "Gem Mint" designation, which is why the BGS boundary sits below 10. A BGS 9 ("Mint") does not count toward gem rate.
</Note>

Below the gem rate percentage, the tile lists your **top 4 grading companies** by volume with proportional horizontal bars so you can see your grader mix at a glance. If you use more than four graders, the remaining companies are collapsed and not individually shown.

### Collection Stats

This tile covers image coverage and release-era distribution.

- **Image Coverage %** — the share of card types that have at least a front image attached.
- **Unique Card Types** — mirrors the KPI tile for quick cross-reference.
- **Release-Year Era Breakdown** — a six-row table showing how many cards fall into each collector era:

| Era | Years |
|---|---|
| Boomer | Pre-1960 |
| Vintage | 1960–1980 |
| Junk Wax | 1981–1993 |
| Nostalgia | 1994–1998 |
| Modern | 1999–2017 |
| Ultra-Modern | 2018+ |

Cards without a known release year are not counted in the era table.

### Attributes

A four-count tile listing the number of cards in your collection that carry each of the four major attribute flags:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Rookie Cards" icon="star">
    Cards with the **RC** (rookie card) flag set.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Autographs" icon="pen-nib">
    Cards with the **AU** (autograph) flag set.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Relics" icon="shirt">
    Cards with the **MEM** (memorabilia/relic) flag set.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Serial Numbered" icon="hashtag">
    Cards that carry an individually stamped serial number — for example, 047/250 physically printed on the card. Cards where only the print run is known but no individual number is stamped are not counted here.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Info>
  The **SN** badge means the card has an individually stamped serial number (e.g., 12/100 printed on the card). A card with only a known print run but no individual serial stamp shows a **PR** badge instead and is not counted in the Serial Numbered attribute tile.
</Info>

## Section 3 — Value/Cards Chart

The bottom section of the Dashboard is a fully interactive chart that plots your collection's history.

### Controls

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Category Dropdown" icon="filter">
    Filter the chart to a single sports category or view **All Categories** combined.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Time Range Selector" icon="calendar">
    Choose from **7 Days**, **1 Month**, **1 Year**, or **All Time** to zoom the x-axis.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Value / Cards Toggle" icon="toggle-on">
    Switch between plotting **Total Value** (dollar amount) and **Total Cards** (count). Both datasets exist for every data point — toggling just changes which line is foregrounded.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Period KPI Row

Directly below the chart, a three-cell row summarizes the visible window:

| Cell | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Period Start** | The value or card count at the left edge of the selected time range |
| **Period End** | The value or card count at the right edge of the selected time range |
| **Change** | The absolute and percentage difference between Period Start and Period End |

<Tip>
  Use the **7 Days** range after running a large import to quickly confirm that both your card count and total value moved in the expected direction.
</Tip>
