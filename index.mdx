CardVault is a local web application that puts your entire trading card collection at your fingertips. Whether you're tracking 500 cards or 56,000+, CardVault helps you browse and search your inventory, manage curated collections, look up real eBay prices, analyze your portfolio, and produce monthly reports — all from your browser.

## What's Inside CardVault

CardVault covers every aspect of serious card collecting:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Import your inventory">
    Upload your Excel workbooks through the Import page. CardVault reads your data, reconciles values intelligently, and logs every change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse and search">
    Navigate by sport category, search by player name, and apply filters for graded cards, autographs, rookies, relics, and serial-numbered cards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Price your cards">
    Use the eBay price lookup tool to fetch real sold-listing data, or set up default value rules to price entire groups of cards at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track and analyze">
    Monitor your collection's total value over time, explore your Top 100 most valuable cards, follow your CardVault 50 custom index, and generate detailed monthly reports.
  </Step>
</Steps>
