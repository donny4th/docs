CardVault runs locally on your machine and opens in your browser — no internet connection required. This guide walks you through launching the app and importing your first inventory workbook so you can start exploring your collection right away.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure you have:

- CardVault installed and ready to launch on your machine
- At least one `.xlsm` inventory workbook ready to upload

## Launch and Import

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start CardVault">
    Launch CardVault using the startup shortcut or script provided during installation. Once it's running, you'll see a confirmation that the app is available at:

    ```
    http://127.0.0.1:5000
    ```

    Leave the CardVault process running in the background — closing it will shut down the app.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open CardVault in your browser">
    Open your browser and go to:

    ```
    http://127.0.0.1:5000
    ```

    The CardVault home page will load. You can switch between dark and light themes using the theme toggle in the navigation bar.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Navigate to Import Inventory">
    Click **Import** in the top navigation bar (or go directly to [http://127.0.0.1:5000/import](http://127.0.0.1:5000/import)).

    The Import page lets you upload an `.xlsm` workbook to populate or refresh your inventory for a given sport category.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select your workbook and upload">
    On the Import page:

    1. Use the **Workbook** dropdown to select the sport or category that matches your file (e.g., *Basketball (excluding Dirk)*, *Baseball*, *Hockey*).
    2. Click **Choose File** and select your `.xlsm` workbook.
    3. Click **Import** to begin the upload.

    CardVault will process the file, wipe the existing cards for that category, and import all rows from your workbook. For a full explanation of how values are reconciled during import, see [Importing Inventory](/importing-inventory).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm in Import History">
    After the import completes, scroll down to the **Import History** table or navigate to the history section of the Import page. You should see a new entry with:

    - The workbook type you selected
    - A timestamp for the import
    - A count of cards imported

    Your inventory is now live. Head to [http://127.0.0.1:5000/inventory](http://127.0.0.1:5000/inventory) to start browsing your cards.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If a page looks out of date after the app updates, do a hard refresh with **Ctrl+Shift+R** (Windows/Linux) or **Cmd+Shift+R** (Mac) to force the browser to reload all cached assets.
</Tip>
